# What a keep does not prove

A kept candidate means one thing: against a frozen contract, on one machine, a number moved the right way and no hard gate failed. Everything below is a claim that result does not support. They are collected here because each of them is easy to imply by accident once a loop starts producing green numbers.

## Lab numbers are not product evidence

An accessibility number from these loops, or from the product-web adapter, is not WCAG AA conformance: the adapter's keyboard traversal checks at most eight controls, and its contrast sampling handles only simple opaque colours. SEO and GEO results are local lint and local scorers, and no rankings or AI citations are proven by them. Lab scores do not prove customer demand, and nothing in a run moves a product bet gate or counts as promotion evidence. If the question needs live users, an A/B platform answers it and this cannot.

That boundary is why the [case studies](case-studies.md) keep their claim lines next to their numbers. A readiness score of 100.00 says the work was done, not that an answer engine cites the page.

## The training example is not model parity

The [tiny Candle example](../../examples/nanochat-candle/README.md) runs a bounded CPU/f32 training experiment. It is not nanochat, CUDA or H100 training parity, and the [parity report](../reference/training-parity.md) records where it differs, including a known optimizer discrepancy.

## Nothing is isolated

There is no OS-level network isolation or sandbox for evaluator or agent processes, and descendant processes are not cleaned up. `requires_network` and `[authority]` are declarations that never grant anything, so run untrusted evaluators and agents inside a sandbox you supply. A timeout kills the evaluator the runner started, not the processes that evaluator spawned.

## Nothing leaves your machine

autoresearch-rs does not deploy or publish anything. A keep moves a local ref, and `export` writes a redacted bundle to a directory you chose, where the redaction is a bounded defence and not proof that no secret remains. The [report and export reference](../reference/report-and-export.md) says what each bundle contains.

## It is not autonomous

It is not autonomous orchestration: each `run` call advances one candidate, and nothing schedules the next call for you. Promotion is a separate human decision, taken with the report in front of you, and described in [take a kept result](../how-to/take-a-kept-result.md).

## What the tool does enforce

The list above is about claims. For what the runner does guarantee, and where those guarantees stop, read [how a run decides](how-a-run-decides.md) for the decision order and [what a run does to your repository](../reference/repository-effects.md) for the Git effects.
